## What this does and does not guarantee

It guarantees every fight loads a creature the engine has, of the right
kind: a leader for a leader, a security robot for a security robot, an
insect for an insect. That is what fixes the Salvation prologue, where
Matrix Cubed's `PURGE COMMANDO` and `PURGE WARRIOR` collided with
Countdown's `HEXADILLO` and `SAND SQUID` and put poisonous desert wildlife
in front of a starting party.

It does **not** guarantee the numbers match. The DOS records show
`PURGE COMMANDO` and `PURGE WARRIOR` carrying identical stat bytes
somewhat above `TERRINE WARRIOR`'s, so the substitute is in the right band
rather than equal to it. Verifying that properly needs the 214-byte Genesis
record mapped field by field against the 259-byte DOS one, which is also
exactly what transplanting Matrix Cubed's own 63 records requires. Until
then, treat encounter difficulty as approximate.
